PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Polish Keyboard ("we", "us" or "our") provides the Polish Keyboard iOS app. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

Information we process

  • Account information. You sign in through Sign in with Apple. We process the account identifier supplied through our authentication service. Apple may provide an email address or name only when you choose to share it.
  • Usage and subscription information. We store your daily usage count and subscription entitlement status so we can apply the free limit and Pro benefits.
  • Text you choose to polish. When you tap the polish action, the selected text and style choice are sent to our server and then to DeepSeek to generate a revised version. We do not intentionally store this request text in our application database.
  • Account-deletion credential. For users who sign in with Apple, we securely retain the Apple refresh token provided during sign-in solely to revoke that Apple authorization when the user deletes the account.

How we use information

We use the information above to authenticate you, provide text-polishing results, enforce usage limits, provide and restore subscription benefits, secure the service, and respond to support requests.

Service providers and transfers

We use Supabase to provide authentication, database and server-function infrastructure, Apple for sign-in and in-app subscriptions, and DeepSeek to process text that you choose to polish. These providers may process information on our behalf in locations outside your country. We do not sell personal information or use the text you submit for advertising.

Retention and deletion

We retain account, usage and subscription records while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account inside the app. Deletion removes your app profile, usage records, subscription records and stored Apple sign-in credential, and requests revocation of the Apple authorization. Deleting the app account does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage or cancel that subscription through Apple.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Polish Keyboard is not directed to children under the age at which they may independently consent to data processing in their location. Do not use the app if you do not meet that age requirement.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version is published on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact polishkeyboard.support@gmail.com.

隐私政策

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 15 日

Polish Keyboard（“我们”）提供 Polish Keyboard iOS App。本政策说明你使用 App 时我们如何处理信息。

我们处理的信息

  • 账户信息。你通过“通过 Apple 登录”使用 App。我们处理认证服务提供的账户标识符；只有在你选择共享时，Apple 才可能提供邮箱或姓名。
  • 用量和订阅信息。我们保存每日使用次数及订阅权益状态，以执行免费额度并提供 Pro 权益。
  • 你选择润色的文字。当你点击润色时，所选文字和风格会发送到我们的服务器，再发送至 DeepSeek 生成改写结果。我们不会有意将这些请求文字保存在 App 数据库中。
  • 账户删除凭证。对于通过 Apple 登录的用户，我们安全保存登录时获得的 Apple 刷新令牌，仅用于用户删除账户时撤销该 Apple 授权。

用途、服务商与删除

我们使用上述信息完成登录、提供润色、执行额度、提供及恢复订阅权益、保障服务安全和回复支持请求。我们使用 Supabase 提供认证、数据库和服务器功能，Apple 提供登录与 App 内订阅，DeepSeek 处理你主动选择润色的文字。这些服务商可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理信息。我们不出售个人信息，也不会把提交的文字用于广告。

账户存续期间，我们保留账户、用量和订阅记录。你可在 App 内永久删除账户；这会删除你的 App 资料、用量、订阅记录和存储的 Apple 登录凭证，并请求撤销 Apple 授权。删除 App 账户不会取消 Apple 订阅，请通过 Apple 管理或取消订阅。

安全、儿童与联系我们

我们采取合理的技术和组织措施保护信息，但任何互联网服务都无法保证绝对安全。本 App 不面向未达到所在地可独立同意数据处理年龄的儿童。如有隐私问题或请求，请联系 polishkeyboard.support@gmail.com。我们可能在 App 更新时修订本政策，最新版本会发布在本页面。
